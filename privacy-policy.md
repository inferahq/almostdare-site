# AlmostDare privacy policy

Effective date: the day this page is published (the owner fills it in).

AlmostDare is made by Infera. It puts a short challenge in front of the apps you choose. This policy explains what AlmostDare knows, where it keeps it, and how you stay in control.

## The short version

- AlmostDare collects no personal data and shares nothing with anyone.
- Everything stays on your phone. AlmostDare has no internet access at all.
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting.

## Accessibility Service

AlmostDare uses Android's Accessibility Service to notice when an app you chose comes to the front, so it can show your challenge first. AlmostDare asks for your consent on a separate screen before it sends you to Android's settings to turn it on, and it does nothing until you agree.

- It listens only for "a new window opened" and learns only which app the window belongs to (its package name, for example `com.instagram.android`).
- It cannot read what is on your screen, what you type, or your messages. Its settings don't allow it to see window content.
- The package name is compared with your list of chosen apps on your phone. It is never sent anywhere. AlmostDare keeps it only while that app's challenge is on screen, so a crash or an update can't skip the challenge, and forgets it when the challenge closes.
- If you turn on "Ask again while using" for a folder, the challenge can also appear while you are using one of its apps.
- It never taps, types, or changes anything for you, and it never stops you from opening Android's settings, an app store, or uninstalling any app. These system apps can't be chosen at all.
- You can turn it off anytime in Android's accessibility settings.

## What is stored on your phone

Only what AlmostDare needs to work, in its private app storage:

- The apps you chose, your folders, and their challenge settings (which challenge, grid size and style, how long an app stays open, "Ask again while using").
- Your theme and appearance choice.
- Whether you finished the introduction and agreed to the accessibility disclosure.
- Whether reminders are on, and when AlmostDare last reminded you that protection was off (so it never reminds you too often).
- While a challenge is on screen: which app it is for (see above).

How long an unlocked app stays open is kept in memory only and is gone when AlmostDare stops.

AlmostDare reads the list of apps installed on your phone only to show them when you choose apps. That list isn't saved. When you uninstall an app you had chosen, AlmostDare removes it from your list.

This data is excluded from Google's cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it never leaves your phone that way either.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Accessibility Service | To notice which app opens (above) |
| Notifications | To tell you when protection turns off, and, if you leave reminders on, to remind you now and then until it's back on |
| Vibration | A short buzz for a right or wrong answer |
| Run at startup | To check protection after your phone restarts |

AlmostDare does not ask for internet access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

## Deleting your data

Clear AlmostDare's storage in Android's settings, or uninstall it. Either removes everything; there is no copy anywhere else.

## Age

AlmostDare is meant for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. AlmostDare will never start collecting data without asking you first.
